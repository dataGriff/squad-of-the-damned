# itch.io page copy (#49)

**Title:** Squad of the Damned
**Tagline:** Four kids. Five monsters. One night each.

---

The new family on Elm St never comes out by day. The postman howls at the full moon. Something in the storm drain is eating the frogs. Nobody over twelve believes a word of it, so the squad is going out tonight.

**Squad of the Damned** is an 8-bit platformer where you play one body with four shapes. Swap between the kids on the fly:

- **Casey** — slingshot. Pebbles are free, silver is not.
- **Mo** — garlic bombs. Stuns everything in the cloud.
- **Bruno** — pushes crates, punches through cracked walls.
- **Pip** — fits through vents, double jumps.

Every night ends with a classic monster, and every monster has a weakness — but it only works once you have found its page of the **Monster Manual**. Knowledge is the power-up.

### Features
- Five nights, five bosses, a timed portal finale
- 256×240, 16px tiles, four colours per sprite: real NES constraints
- Chiptune soundtrack on a four-channel tracker, with a per-night theme
- Coyote time, jump buffering and all the other things that make a jump feel right
- Keyboard, gamepad and touch; fully remappable
- CRT filter, hold-to-jump, reduced flash
- Runs in the browser; desktop build via Tauri

### Controls
Arrows / WASD move · Z jump · X ability · C swap · Enter pause · Up + X fires silver · hold X to charge

### Retro manual
Download the PDF manual (`docs/manual.pdf`, built with `npm run manual:pdf`) — it matches the in-game bestiary, silhouettes and all.
