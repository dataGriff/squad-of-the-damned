# Night 5 — The Storm Drain

**Theme** storm drain · **Boss** The Gill Creature · **Song** `night5` · **Required** Bruno, Pip

## Beats
1. Outfall, lamp, frogs, water gap. The water level is set for the whole night (`water: true`).
2. Lamp, crawl, **vent** with the Gill Creature's page, bats.
3. **Cracked wall** (Bruno), lamp, water gap, frogs, steps.
4. Secret room (silver), heart, arena: a flooded chamber with a sluice gate on the right wall.

## Gimmick
Reading the manual *before* the fight: the page says "drain the water", and the arena has an obvious gate only Bruno can break. Without the page you can break the gate but the creature does not care.

## Boss: The Gill Creature
Lurks at the surface, lunges out of the water at you, or spits three homing bubbles (pop them with any shot). Three punches from Bruno break the sluice; the water drains a row per second. Once drained **and the page is known** he is beached: flops along the floor, slow, permanently vulnerable. Phase 2 (below half) thrashes faster and spits while leaping.

Clearing Night 5 unlocks *Midnight: The Portal* in the treehouse.

## Pages
- *The Gill Creature* — behind the vent (requires Pip).
