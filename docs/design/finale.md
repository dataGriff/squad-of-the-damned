# Midnight — The Portal (#28)

**Theme** graveyard · **Song** `finale` · **Required** all four · **Timer** 90 s

One screen. The portal stands in the middle, the redeemed Giant beside it holding it shut, and bats breathe out of it every four seconds. Four seals around the graveyard each need one kid:

| Seal | Kid | How |
|---|---|---|
| top-left ledge | Casey | hit it with a pebble or silver |
| right ledge | Mo | a garlic bomb's stun cloud touches it |
| behind the vent, bottom right | Pip | walk into it |
| pressure plate on the left, behind the crate | Bruno | push the crate onto it |

The HUD clock counts down from 11:30 to midnight. All four seals lit → the portal collapses, "THE PORTAL IS CLOSED!", and the credits roll. Midnight → "THE PORTAL OPENS", everything resets and you try again (lives do not count here).
