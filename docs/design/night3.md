# Night 3 — Full Moon over Maple Drive

**Theme** suburb · **Boss** The Wolfman · **Song** `night3` · **Required** Casey, Pip, Bruno

## Beats
1. Street, lamp, rats, spike pit, bats. Fast pace: this is the POC night polished (#25).
2. Lamp. Crate ledge, then a **silver cache** — the night is about ammo.
3. Rats, **vent** with the Wolfman's page behind it.
4. High silver ledge (crate or Pip's double jump), lamp, steps, another silver cache.
5. Secret room (*Rats* page), arena on the cul-de-sac.

## Gimmick
Resource management. Pebbles are infinite and useless against him; silver is finite (5 to start, +3 per cache). The level hands out exactly enough silver to win with a couple of misses, and the secret room tops you up.

## Boss: The Wolfman
Phase 1 paces, then crouches (`!`) and lunges across the arena. After every lunge he is dazed for 1.2 s — the vulnerability window. Silver (hold **up** + fire) only lands in that window and only once the page is known; otherwise it sparks off. Pebbles never hurt him. Phase 2 (below half) lunges faster and every other cycle he **howls**, shaking the screen and summoning two bats.

## Pages
- *The Wolfman* — behind the vent (requires Pip).
- *Rats* — secret room.
