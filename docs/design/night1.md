# Night 1 — The House on Elm St

**Theme** mansion · **Boss** The Count · **Song** `night1` · **Required** Mo, Pip

## Beats
1. Front lawn, lamp. Rats on the drive teach the slingshot (any kid can jump them).
2. Porch steps (`STEPS`): one-way platforms, drop-through.
3. Hall with bats: first flying enemy; bats swoop when you are level with them.
4. Lamp. Dumbwaiter crawl (`CRAWL`): crouch under the ceiling, a rat waits inside.
5. **The vent** (`VENT_PAGE`): a wall with a grate at the bottom. Only Pip fits. The Count's page is behind it. This is the night's gating moment and the first time the swap button matters.
6. Heart on a ledge, spike pit, more bats.
7. Secret room above two vents (field notes: *Bats* page, silver).
8. Lamp, pillared ballroom, arena.

## Gimmick
Learning that the squad is one body with four shapes: the vent cannot be passed by anyone but Pip, so the swap is taught by a wall rather than a tutorial box.

## Boss: The Count
Phase 1 floats and swoops across the arena after a cape telegraph (`!`). Garlic bombs do nothing until the page is found; after that a bomb stuns him for 2.5 s and *anything* hurts while he is dazed ("stun him then hit hard"). Phase 2 (below half) he scatters into four bats for a few seconds — kill them or wait him out — then reforms and swoops faster.

## Pages
- *The Count* — behind the vent (main path, requires Pip).
- *Bats* — secret room.
