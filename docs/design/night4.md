# Night 4 — Under the Museum

**Theme** sewer · **Boss** The Mummy · **Song** `night4` · **Required** Casey, Bruno, Pip

## Beats
1. Service tunnel, lamp. **Water gap** with a bog frog: water slows you and halves gravity.
2. Frogs, lamp, **cracked wall** (Bruno).
3. Rats, ladder shaft, crawl space, **vent** (Pip) with the Mummy's page.
4. Lamp, second water gap, bats, heart, arena in the flooded gallery.

## Gimmick
Everything learned so far, in water. Frogs can't turn mid-hop so you dodge by waiting.

## Boss: The Mummy
Shuffles toward you. When you are level with him he winds up (`!`) and fires a bandage grapple across the arena; if it touches you it reels you in for a bite. Three valves hang in the arena: a pebble opens one for three seconds and water pours down. While he stands in the spray **and his page is known** he is soaked: slower, no grapple, vulnerable to anything. Phase 2 (below half) shuffles faster and reaches further.

## Pages
- *The Mummy* — behind the vent (requires Pip).
- *Bog Frogs* — floating over the first frog field.
